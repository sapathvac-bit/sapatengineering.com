About SapatEngineering

A Dubai-based HVAC and ventilation engineering company committed to delivering world-class climate solutions across the UAE.

Our Story

Engineering Comfort for Dubai's Demanding Climate

SapatEngineering was founded with a clear purpose: to bring premium-quality HVAC engineering to the UAE market. Having seen too many buildings in Dubai suffer from poorly designed or incorrectly installed climate systems, our founders — experienced HVAC engineers — set out to build a company where engineering excellence comes first.

Over the years we have built a reputation for technical accuracy, professional delivery, and outstanding client service. From a single villa to a multi-tower development, we apply the same rigorous engineering approach to every project.

Today, SapatEngineering serves residential, commercial, hospitality, and industrial clients across Dubai, with a team of senior engineers, AutoCAD designers, project managers, and certified installation technicians.
