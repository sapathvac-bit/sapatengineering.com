Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

SapatEngineering ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information you provide when using our website sapatengineering.com.

2. Data We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide through our contact forms, including: full name, phone number, email address, preferred contact date/time, and project details. We also collect standard website analytics data (page views, browser type, approximate location) via cookies.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to respond to your enquiry, provide quotations, and communicate about your project. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties except as required to process your enquiry (e.g., our email service provider).

4. Data Retention

We retain contact data for a maximum of 3 years, after which it is securely deleted. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting sapat.hvac@gmail.com.

5. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies for functionality and analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; this will not affect your ability to use the website.
