Smoke Extraction & Control Systems in Dubai

Smoke extraction systems are a critical life-safety requirement for virtually all commercial buildings in Dubai. They allow occupants to evacuate safely during a fire by controlling the spread of toxic smoke, and they assist firefighters in locating and combating fires.

SapatEngineering designs, supplies, and installs smoke extraction systems in full compliance with Dubai Civil Defence regulations, NFPA standards, and BS EN 12101 specifications.

Smoke Control Applications

  • Basement and multi-level car park ventilation/smoke extraction
  • Corridor pressurisation systems for high-rise evacuation
  • Staircase pressurisation systems
  • Atrium smoke management systems
  • Shopping mall and retail smoke control
  • Hotel and residential tower smoke shaft systems

System Types We Install

  • Mechanical smoke extraction with dedicated fans and ductwork
  • Natural smoke ventilation with automatic AOVs
  • Pressure differential systems for escape routes
  • Combined natural and mechanical systems
  • BMS-integrated smoke control with alarm interface

Dubai Civil Defence Approval

All our smoke extraction designs are prepared by qualified engineers and submitted for Dubai Civil Defence approval. We manage the full approval process on your behalf and ensure the installed system passes final inspection and commissioning.
