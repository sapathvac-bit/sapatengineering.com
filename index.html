FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our HVAC and ventilation services in Dubai, UAE.

Ask a Question
Do you provide a free site survey?
Yes — completely free and no-obligation. Our engineer visits your property, assesses requirements, and provides a detailed proposal within 48 hours across all Dubai districts.
How long does HVAC installation take?
A single split AC takes 4–6 hours. Multi-room or ducted systems take 2–5 days. Full building HVAC projects are scheduled individually after the design phase is complete.
Are you compliant with Dubai Green Building standards?
Yes. All designs consider Dubai's Estidama Pearl Rating and Green Building Regulations. We can assist with documentation for sustainability certification.
Do you offer Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)?
Yes. Comprehensive AMC packages include scheduled inspections, filter changes, performance checks, and priority fault response — keeping your system at peak efficiency year-round.
Which areas of Dubai do you cover?
All Dubai districts: Downtown, Business Bay, DIFC, JBR, Marina, Palm Jumeirah, Jumeirah, Deira, Bur Dubai, Al Quoz, and more. We also serve Abu Dhabi and Sharjah on request.
Which HVAC brands do you work with?
Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric, LG, Samsung, Carrier, Trane, York, Gree, Midea, Panasonic, Fujitsu, and many more. Recommendations are always needs-based, never commission-driven.
