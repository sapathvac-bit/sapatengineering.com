Why Proper Ventilation Matters in Dubai

Dubai's desert climate presents unique ventilation challenges. Summer temperatures regularly exceed 45°C, and coastal humidity can reach 90%+. Without a properly engineered ventilation system, buildings suffer from poor indoor air quality, mould risk, overloaded AC systems, and non-compliance with building codes.

A well-designed ventilation system works in harmony with your HVAC to maintain healthy CO₂ levels, remove cooking and bathroom odours, and introduce filtered fresh air — all without compromising your air conditioning efficiency.

Design Standards We Follow

  • ASHRAE 62.1 — Ventilation for Acceptable Indoor Air Quality
  • Dubai Municipality Building Regulations
  • Dubai Civil Defence life-safety requirements
  • Estidama Pearl Building Rating System (Green Building)
  • British Standard BS EN 13779
