Professional AC Cleaning in Dubai

Dubai's extreme heat, coastal humidity, and frequent sandstorms create the perfect conditions for dust, mould, and bacteria to accumulate inside air conditioning units. A dirty AC doesn't just underperform — it circulates contaminated air throughout your home or office, affecting health and significantly increasing electricity bills.

Sapat Engineering provides professional AC cleaning and deep sanitisation for all AC types across Dubai. Our certified technicians use industry-grade equipment and eco-friendly antibacterial solutions to restore your AC to peak performance.

Why AC Cleaning Is Essential in Dubai

Dubai's environment is particularly harsh on air conditioning systems:

  • Desert dust and sand clog filters and evaporator coils within weeks
  • High humidity promotes mould and bacteria growth inside units
  • A dirty AC can consume up to 25% more electricity
  • Blocked drainage causes water leaks and ceiling damage
  • Contaminated air aggravates allergies, asthma, and respiratory conditions
  • Neglected units fail faster, requiring costly early replacement

How Often Should You Clean Your AC?

In Dubai's environment we recommend:

  • Every 1 month — filter cleaning (can be done by owner)
  • Every 3 months — professional deep clean, especially during peak summer
  • Every 6–12 months — full chemical wash and sanitisation
  • After sandstorms — immediate filter check and clean
